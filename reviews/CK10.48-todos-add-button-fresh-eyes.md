# CK10.48 — todos' list scrolled to its end, read cold in both appearances

## What was reviewed

Headless renders at one pixel per point, light and dark: the whole Todos
window (650×600 under a 32 px title-bar strip) holding twelve todos,
scrolled to its end with the mouse wheel at the When scrolling setting. The
reader saw no source and no packet, was given the Scrollbar entry of
DOMAIN.md and the full text of reviews/misreads.md, and was asked what a
developer who uses macOS every day would complain about in how the end of
the list is drawn: whether every delete mark can be clicked, whether the
last row ends clear of the add button, and whether the spacing below the
last row looks right.

## The reviewer's reply, verbatim

I found three things to file. Every delete mark is fully visible and away from both the scroll bar and the add button, and the last row ends 25 px above the add button. The spacing below the last row is wider than the gap between rows, but it is the room kept free for the add button, not an error. Both schemes have identical geometry; only the colours differ.

**Geometry at the end of the list (same in light and dark)**
- **Rows:** they repeat every 64 px. Row 12 ("Todo number 12") has its content at y 496–519, and its cell runs to about y 540.
- **Delete marks:** every X is 22×22 at x 595–616. They are the same size and position in all eight visible rows, none is clipped, and nothing overlaps them. The X ends 26 px before the scroll bar's thumb (x 642–647). The thumb at "When scrolling" lies over the rows' trailing edge without a lane of its own, as the Scrollbar entry says, and it lies over nothing that can be clicked. So every row's X can be clicked. Hit-target size can't be judged from a still.
- **Last row and the add button:** the add button is a 40 px disc at x 594–633, y 544–583. It sits directly below row 12's X, in the same column. Row 12's drawn content ends at y 519, so the gap is 25 px. Measured from the row's own cell (to y 540), the gap is about 4 px. So the last row ends clear of the button.
- **Spacing below the last row:** from row 12's content (y 519) to the window's bottom edge (y 600) is 80 px of blank space, with only the button in it. Between rows the blank gap is 40 px. Counting from the cell, there are about 60 px below the last row, which matches the button's 40 px plus its 16 px bottom margin. The button's trailing margin is also 16 px (633→650), so its margins are even. Nothing else is drawn in that area.
- **Scroll bar:** the thumb is at x 642–647 (6 wide, 2 px from the edge) and y 220–597. It ends 3 px from the bottom and starts well below the title strip. Its length, 378 px, roughly matches about 568 px visible out of about 830 px of content. There is no lane, which is correct for this setting.
- **Top edge:** row 5's cell begins at about y 28 and is cut off at the 32 px title strip. Only empty row inset is lost; its text and checkbox start at y 48. That is ordinary clipping, as the entry requires.

**What I would file**
1. **The unchecked checkbox is nearly invisible in light.** Its 1 px border is (243,243,243) on (255,255,255), almost no contrast; on a 1x frame it reads as a blank space before the text. In dark the border is (44,51,56) on (30,30,30), also very faint. A macOS checkbox has a clearly visible grey border with a white fill, so a daily Mac user would notice this first. Rows 5, 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12 show it. The box itself is at x 27–42 (16 px), and the text starts at x 58.
2. **A round floating "+" is a Material pattern.** No Mac list window has a floating action button. The platform puts Add in the toolbar or in a +/− button bar along the list's bottom edge. The 60 px kept free at the list's end exists only because of this button, so it is the root of the "extra space below the last row." This falls under "platform wins over Material."
3. **The delete marks are too prominent.** Each is a full-accent (0,122,255) 22 px X on every row, about as large as the 13pt text beside it. On macOS a delete per row is usually a muted grey glyph, often shown only on hover, or a swipe or context-menu action. Eight blue Xs in a column outweigh the content. This is a judgement call, not a geometry defect.

**Minor, unverified:** the 8 px gap between the thumb (x 642) and the button's trailing edge (x 633) makes that corner look crowded beside the button. It is not an overlap.

**Not filed, because they are recorded misreads:** the missing window chrome and traffic lights, the Roboto font, the missing shadow under the button, and any hue impressions. The completed rows' text greys measure (128,128,128) in light and (154,154,154) in dark, which is the intended dimmed look for completed rows.
