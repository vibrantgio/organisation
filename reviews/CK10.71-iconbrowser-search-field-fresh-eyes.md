# CK10.71 — iconbrowser's search field on content, read cold in both appearances

## What was reviewed

Iconbrowser's six window goldens (no query, "folder", a query that
matches nothing; light and dark) at 1000×700, one pixel per point, against
the two captures `reference/macos/systemsettings-add-input-source-search-{light,dark}.png`.
The reader was given the Search field entry and the section "What the search
field on content measures" verbatim, and the full text of
`reviews/misreads.md`, and asked what a developer who uses the platform every
day would complain about.

## The reader's reply, verbatim

> I compared the six frames with the two System Settings captures, sampling pixels with PIL. The field on the window's fill (outer box x 12–987, y 44–71) is the bordered field on content, not a chrome recess, in both appearances. Most measured rows match. These are the deviations a daily Mac user would notice.
>
> **Where it agrees with the measurement**
> - **No fill of its own.** The inside reads `#ffffff` light and `#1e1e1e` dark, the same as the window fill at (500,58) and (700,50). This matches the "no fill" row.
> - **Light edge.** One pixel `#f3f3f3` at y=44 and y=71 (x=500) and at x=12 and x=987 (y=58). It matches to the byte.
> - **Ends fully rounded.** Column 12 is touched only over y 53–62, and row 44 ramps from `#fcfcfc` at x=21 to full `#f3f3f3` at x=25. That fits a radius of about 14, half the height.
> - **Leading inset.** The looking glass starts at x=20, 8 px from the outer edge, matching the measured 8.
> - **Prompt colour.** "Search" bottoms out at `#808080` light and peaks at `#9a9a9a` dark, both exact. The looking glass is `#818181` light and `#9a9a9a` dark, so the mark and the prompt are one colour, as measured.
> - **Vertical centring.** The prompt band sits centred in the field.
>
> **Complaints**
>
> 1. **Dark edge has a blue cast** (all three dark frames). The edge reads `#2c3338` (44,51,56) at (500,44), (500,71), (12,58) and (987,58). That is on a neutral `#1e1e1e` fill, where the platform reads a neutral `#282828`. On an untinted window this is a bluish hairline, and it is the one hue in the frame. The reference says the tinted and untinted readings don't settle each other. In these frames, though, the fill is untinted and the edge is tinted, so the pairing doesn't match either capture.
> 2. **Height 28 instead of 26, and text a size too large** (all frames). The field runs y 44–71, 28 px against the platform's 26. "Search" spans y 52–63 light and 52–64 dark, so the cap band is about 11–12 px against 10. Typed "folder" is equally large. This is the open item the reference names, and it is visible: the field and its text are one step bigger than System Settings'.
> 3. **Looking glass a little small.** It is 12×12 light (x 20–31, y 53–64) and 13×13 dark (x 20–32, y 53–65), against 14×13. The gap to the prompt is 5 px light (x 32–36) and 4 px dark, against 4. These are minor, but the light frame is off by a pixel.
> 4. **Clear mark too heavy, and inset differently from the glass** (some-match and no-match). It is a solid disc at x 960–972, y 51–64, painted in the typed-text colour: `#272727` light, `#dddddd` dark (sampled at 962,58). The platform's clear mark is a mid-grey disc that sits back from the text, so this one shouts on white. Its trailing inset is 15 px (972 to 987), while the glass's leading inset is 8, so the field's two ends look unbalanced.
> 5. **No search highlight on the matches** (some-match light and dark). Between y 80 and 290 there is not one pixel with any chroma: 216 colours light, 192 dark, all grey. The "folder" labels and names are filtered but not marked. The entry says the field "marks what it finds with the search highlight". Either the catalogue owes a highlight on the matched part of each name, or the entry needs to state that a filtering field marks nothing.
> 6. **Empty state too faint** (no-match). "No icons match "zzzz"" peaks at `#bdbdbd` on white and `#565656` on `#1e1e1e`. Both are well below the `#808080` and `#9a9a9a` prompt colours, so the empty state reads as disabled, lighter than placeholder text, at 24 px.
> 7. **Section headers in the no-match frame disagree.** "Vibrant Gio marks" disappears, while "Material Design icons — 0 icons" stays (y ≈ 97). One section hides and the other shows a zero count. A Mac user expects either both to stay with counts or both to go.
> 8. **Field width.** At 976 px the field spans the whole page. The platform's field on content is a fixed, modest width (209 px in the sheet). This is a taste call more than a defect, but a page-wide capsule reads as a web search bar.
>
> I did not report anything covered by the recorded misreads: no window chrome, no focus ring, Roboto instead of SF, and Material icons.
