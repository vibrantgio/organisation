# CK10.35 — mindchat's transcript, vaultview's note column and themer's style column at When scrolling and Always, read cold in both appearances

## What was reviewed

Twelve whole-window renders at 1x: mindchat (1024 × 768), vaultview (1100 × 800) and themer (1200 × 900), each in light and dark at the platform setting When scrolling with nothing scrolled and at Always. The reviewer was briefed with `reviews/misreads.md`, read whole, given no checklist, and asked what a developer on macOS would complain about in the three scrolling columns.

## The reply, verbatim

The most serious problem a macOS developer would raise is that "Always" draws the overlay-style thumb (the knob) instead of a classic scroller, so the setting changes almost nothing. Next come blank scroll-bar lanes in panels that have nothing to scroll, and lane widths that differ from window to window. Coordinates are in 1x pixels from the top-left of each PNG. Every claim marked "measured" comes from sampling pixels with PIL. The rest are visual reads, or comparisons with macOS from memory, and I say so where that applies. All the colours I quote are neutral greys; I make no hue claims. The window chrome, the missing scroll bars at rest under "When scrolling" and the fixed headers above the scrolling areas are covered in misreads.md, so I don't report them.

1. **"Always" shows the "When scrolling" thumb, not a classic scroller, in all three columns and both appearances.** With "Always" set, macOS draws a track the full height of the scroll view, with its own fill, a 1px separator on its leading edge, and the thumb inside it. These renders draw only a bare 6px-wide pill with no track and no separator (measured).
   - Mindchat transcript: the thumb is at x 1015–1020, y 54–644. The lane below it, x 1005–1023 at y 700, is pure background: (255,255,255) in light and (30,30,30) in dark (measured).
   - Vaultview note column: the thumb is at x 766–771, y 203–739. Down x 768 the only other non-background pixel is the divider at y 200 (measured).
   - Themer style list: the thumb is at x 302–307, y 512–693 in light and y 505–660 in dark. Down x 304 nothing else differs from the card fill (measured).
   - Thumb fills are (109,109,109) in light and (159,159,159) in dark for mindchat and vaultview, and (164,167,168) in themer dark (measured). That the result reads as an overlay scroller and not a classic one is my visual read, compared with macOS from memory, not with a stored reference.

2. **"Always" reserves blank lanes in panels that have nothing to scroll.** macOS hides a classic scroller, and leaves no lane, when the content fits. Here the row highlights shrink, but no thumb or track appears (measured). The panels end up with lopsided insets, and in mindchat the titles cut off sooner (visual read).
   - Mindchat sidebar (4 rows): the selected row runs x 18–216 under "When scrolling" and x 18–206 under "Always", a 10px lane. The pencil and close icons move about 9–10px left. "Reactive layouts" becomes "Reactive layo…" and "Cassowary lay…" becomes "Cassowary la…".
   - Vaultview left sidebar: the "Reading list" row runs x 18–237 against x 18–215, a 22px lane. That leaves a 10px inset on the left and about 32px on the right.
   - Vaultview outline panel: the "Reading list" highlight runs x 790–1089 against x 790–1067, again 22px. The Backlinks divider is shortened by the same amount (visual read).
   - All of this holds in both appearances.

3. **Lane reservation is inconsistent from one column to the next.**
   - Mindchat transcript: the column narrows by about 10px and recentres. The code block's right edge moves from x 920 to 915 (measured).
   - Themer list: a 10px lane. The highlight's right edge moves from x 309 to 299, and the "either" tags shift left by about 10px (selection measured, tags a visual read).
   - Vaultview note column: no lane at all. The code block's right edge stays at x ~750 under both settings, and the thumb sits in the margin before the column divider at x 780 (measured).
   - Vaultview's sidebars, which don't scroll, reserve 22px, while its note column, which does, reserves none. Lane widths of 0, 10 and 22px across one design system read as unrelated implementations.

4. **The themer thumb crowds the selection instead of sitting at the edge.** In both appearances the selected row's highlight ends at x 299, and the thumb starts at x 302, a 2px gap. They share the same rows: y 500–519 for the highlight, y 505 onward for the thumb in dark and y 512 onward in light (measured). The thumb ends 12px short of the card's right edge at x ~320 (measured). A classic macOS scroller sits flush against the scroll view's edge, well away from row content (from memory).

5. **Vaultview's horizontal thumb in the code block is drawn over the block's padding, with no lane or track.** Under "Always" a 6px thumb appears at x 286–706, y 736–741 inside the second code block (measured).
   - Its bottom is 1px above the block's bottom border at y 743 (measured).
   - The block's top and bottom edges are the same under both settings, so the bar took no room (measured).
   - It sits about 8px below the "func main() {}" line (visual estimate). A last code line with descenders would come close to touching it.
   - A classic horizontal scroller would add its own track below the code (from memory).
   - Same geometry in light and dark.

6. **Under "When scrolling", nothing at rest shows that the themer list scrolls.** The list is scrolled: the light render opens at "borland" and the dark one at "catppuccin-frappe", and the "Always" thumb is not at the top. Yet the text starts at y 465 and ends at y 816 inside a card spanning y 450–833, with no part-row cut off at either edge (measured).
   - The list looks complete and seems to start at "b" or "c", with nothing to hint at the entries above or below.
   - Mindchat and vaultview show the same thing more mildly: the last visible block ends cleanly above the footer or the composer (visual read).
   - macOS also hides the scroll bars at rest under this setting. It does briefly show them when a view first appears (from memory), which a still frame cannot capture. So this is the least certain complaint.

I found nothing specific to one appearance: each issue above has the same geometry in light and dark (measured for items 1–5). The zoomed crops I used are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-rene-code-w-vibrantgio/3baad769-88d1-4659-9ca5-03f56de1f7bb/scratchpad/review/`.
