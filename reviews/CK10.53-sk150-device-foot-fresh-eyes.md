**Review reply: Device-tab seam, sk150 workbench (after vs before frames, 720 wide, heights 300 and 320, light and dark)**

I measured every frame with PIL. The probe scripts are in /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-rene-code-w-vibrantgio/3baad769-88d1-4659-9ca5-03f56de1f7bb/scratchpad/ck1053/probe/. I found no defect against the Seam or Footer entries in the change under review.

1. **One hairline, correct row, colour and width: yes.**
   - **Where it is:** row 262 in both 300 frames and row 282 in both 320 frames. Each of those rows is one colour across all 720 pixels (x 0–719), and the row is 1 pixel tall: the rows directly above and below it are not that colour across the width.
   - **Colour:** light is (230,230,230), which is #e6e6e6. Dark is (0,0,0), which is #000000. Both match the measured seam colour for their scheme.
   - **Only one:** no other row in any frame has a single-colour run across the full width. The only other long horizontal line is the tab strip's line at row 95 (see item 5).
   - **Footer text:** rows 263 and 264 are empty (window fill) in every frame. The footer's text begins at row 265 in the 300 frames and row 285 in the 320 frames.

2. **Nothing below the hairline, nothing over it.**
   - **Below:** the two rows under the hairline are pure fill, (255,255,255) light and (30,30,30) dark, at every x.
   - **The cut row in the 300 frames:** this is the CP / b-L row. Its ink reaches row 262 in the before frame (159 pixels light, 167 dark, x 20–604). In the after frame that row is entirely seam colour, so the clip ends the grid at the hairline.
   - **The cut row in the 320 frames:** only the top edge of the next row's fields shows, at row 281 (x 84–523). Before the change, row 282 still showed field fill (243,243,243) light and (44,51,56) dark. Now that row is the hairline, and nothing of the cut row appears below it.
   - **Over:** the scroll bar thumb (x 712–717, (109,109,109) light, (159,159,159) dark) runs rows 98–205 at height 300 and 98–234 at height 320. It ends well above the hairline. The hairline row has no other colour in it.

3. **Before vs after, beyond the hairline.**
   - **320 frames:** the only change is row 282, all 720 pixels.
   - **300 frames:** rows 255–261 also differ, in 16 pixels (light) and 33 pixels (dark). Every one of these differs by exactly 1 in a channel, for example (128,128,128)→(129,129,129) at x 102, y 255, and (51,58,63)→(52,58,64) at x 551, y 258. They all sit on the antialiased glyph edges of the cut row.
   - **Likely cause:** the new clip on the grid changes rasterisation of the partly clipped row. This is a note, not a defect: the change is invisible, and nothing in layout, position, colour or text changed.

4. **Footer: no defect against the Footer entry.**
   - **The rule the change implements:** the footer draws no line of its own, and the seam is drawn once, by the region above. That is what the Seam entry asks.
   - **Spacing (a note):** the footer's text has 2 empty rows between the seam and its first ink, and 21 below (ink at rows 265–278 in a 300-row frame). Before the change this was the same, but it went unnoticed with no line at the edge. Now that a seam marks the edge, the text sits close under it. The Footer entry does not rule on padding, so this is a taste item for the owner.

5. **Tab strip line (a note; it may be a defect against Seam, and it is outside this change).**
   - The line under the tab strip is at row 95, x 16–703. It is inset 16 pixels on each side.
   - Its colour outside the accent underline is (230,230,230) in light, but (52,52,52) in dark, not the measured #000000.
   - If this line counts as "the navbar's foot" in the Seam entry, then in dark it is not the measured seam colour. That makes the two horizontal lines in this window differ in dark.
   - If it is the tab strip's own baseline rather than a seam, it is not a finding. I'm not sure which it is, so I leave it to the owner.

6. **Scroll bar at "Always": a note, not a finding.**
   - Only the thumb is drawn. There is no track: columns 712–717 are window fill outside the thumb.
   - The lane starts at row 98 and runs only beside the grid. This matches the recorded misread about fixed regions above or below a scrolling region, so I don't report it as a finding.

No recorded misread applies to items 1–4. I checked hues by pixel sampling, not by eye.
