# CK10.76 — sk150's Device footer, read cold in both appearances

## What was reviewed

Sk150's Device page at 720×420 at one pixel per point, in both appearances,
with the footer of status text drawn at the measures of "What the status bar
measures" in `reference/macos/controls.md`. The reviewer had seen no packet
and no source. The prompt held the Footer and Seam entries of DOMAIN.md, the
section's MEASURED rows, Finder's two fits captures for comparison, and the
full text of `reviews/misreads.md`; the question was what a developer who
uses macOS every day would complain about at the bottom of the page.

## The reviewer's reply, verbatim

Two things at the bottom of the Device page would draw a complaint: the light seam's colour, and what the status text says. The height, the text's colour and the text's place all match the measured rows. All numbers below were read with PIL from the two renders at 720×420.

**What matches (no complaint)**
- **Bar height:** the seam is at y 392 and the fill runs y 393–419. That is 1 + 27 = 28 px to the window's bottom edge, the same as Finder's bar.
- **Seam extent:** a single 1 px row across x 0–719, the full width. It is drawn once.
- **Dark seam colour:** `#000000`, which is Mail's seam and Finder's to the byte.
- **Bar fill:** the same as the content's fill in both schemes, `#ffffff` light and `#1e1e1e` dark. It is untinted and opaque.
- **Text colour:** the darkest text pixel is 130 in light and the lightest is 153 in dark. The measured values are 128 and 153.5, so this matches `secondaryLabelColor`.
- **Text's vertical place:**
  - There are traces at y 401, the cap band is y 402–409 (8 rows) and the commas' tails are at y 410–411. Finder's is y 482–489 with a trace at 481, also 8 rows.
  - The space under the seam is clear for 9 px, y 393–401 counting the trace row as Finder's row does. The baseline is at y 410, 10 px above the bottom edge (y 410–419). Both match Finder's 9 px and 10 px.
  - The cap band's middle, 405.5, is half a pixel off the fill's middle, 406.
- **Text size:** an 8-row cap band fits 11 pt. Monospaced digits cannot be judged from one string in a still.
- **Horizontal place:** the text runs x 102–617, so its middle is 359.5, exactly the window's middle (359.5 in light, 359.0 in dark). Finder's text sits 5 px to the leading side of the middle. The reference lists that offset as open, so it is not a defect here.
- **Square bottom corners:** these are the harness's stand-in, a recorded misread, and are not filed.

**Complaint 1: the light seam is lighter than Finder's status-bar seam**
- **What I see:** the seam at y 392 is `#e6e6e6` in light.
- **What it breaks:** the measured seam row reads `#d9d9d9` for Finder's status bar, 13 levels darker. The render has used Mail's list seam (`separatorColor` over white) for a status bar's top.
- **Against the Seam entry:** the entry lists "the status bar's top" as a seam whose colour is measured from the platform's own windows. The only status-bar measurement on record is `#d9d9d9`.
- **Caveat:** the reference marks this as open ("What is open here"). This is a divergence from the measured row, not yet a settled breach of the entry.

**Complaint 2: the status text is a sentence of help text, not status**
- **What I see:** at x 102–617, y 401–411 the line reads "Link: 9600 baud, Modbus address 1 — changing either needs a power cycle, so this app leaves them alone." It is 516 px wide, more than 70% of the window.
- **Why a Mac developer would object:** Finder's bar holds a short fact ("9 items, 411,13 GB available"). The second half of this line is an explanation of why the app does nothing, which reads as a help note pushed into the status bar.
- **Against the Footer entry:** the entry allows "a column's status text". Only "Link: 9600 baud, Modbus address 1" is status. The explanation belongs beside the settings it concerns, or in help.
- **Second risk:** at this length the line leaves only about 100 px on each side. A narrower window would truncate or clip it, while Finder's short text never comes near the edges.

**Smaller observation: which entry the strip falls under is unclear**
- The strip runs the window's full width under a page, not under a column. Under the Footer entry ("the status bar is the window's own strip, not a column's footer") that makes it the window's status bar rather than the Device page's footer.
- If it shows only on the Device page, it is a page footer drawn to look like the status bar. A Mac user would then expect it to stay put when switching to Monitor or Presets. Whether it does cannot be judged from these two stills.

I found nothing against the Footer entry's height or text-place rule, nor against the seam's extent, its dark colour or its being drawn once.
