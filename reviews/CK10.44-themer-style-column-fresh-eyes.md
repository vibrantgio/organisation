# CK10.44 — themer's style column scrolling as the box's column, read cold in both appearances

## What was reviewed

Four whole-window renders at 1200 × 900, 1x, the golden model: light and dark at the platform setting Always, and light and dark at When scrolling with nothing scrolled. The reviewer was given the Scrollbar entry verbatim and asked what a developer on macOS would complain about. The brief was meant to carry `reviews/misreads.md` inline; it went out with a placeholder in its place, and a follow-up message told the reviewer to read the file whole before replying. The reply does not say whether it did.

## The reply, verbatim

Only the Always frames give a macOS developer something to complain about. The two When scrolling frames hold no thumb, and their rows run to the same trailing edge as the column's other content, so they follow the entry. The Always frames do give the bar a lane, and the bar sits at the column's trailing edge, not inset by the padding. But the lane has no track drawn in it, and its width does not match the bar.

What I measured in the Syntax highlighter style column. The column is x=20..320 and y=451..832 in all four frames. At When scrolling the selected row's highlight runs from x=30 to 310 and no pixels change in x=312..318. At Always the highlight stops at x=300 and the "either" tags move 10px left. The thumb is at x=312..318 (6px wide, ends fully rounded) with 2px to the column edge, and spans y=452..832 for its track. Light: thumb y=513..703, (106,106,106). Dark: thumb y=505..669, (164,167,168).

1. **At Always there is no track, in either scheme.** Every pixel of the lane that the thumb does not cover is the column's own fill: (247,247,247) in light and (42,48,52) in dark. There is no track fill and no hairline at the lane's leading edge. A macOS developer with "Always" set expects a visible track strip separating the scroller from the rows. Here a thumb floats in blank space, so the frame shows a thumb without its track. The entry says the track is measured into the reference per scheme. Either that measurement is missing or it isn't applied.

2. **The lane width and the thumb don't agree.** Measured from where the rows stop (x=300) to the column edge (x=320), the lane is 20px wide. The thumb uses only x=312..318, which leaves an empty 12px strip between the highlight's end and the thumb. A native legacy scroller fills its lane: the knob sits centred in a track whose width is the lane. Here the "lane" is really the rows' 10px padding plus an extra 10px. Compare the 20px with the lane width in the stored reference. It looks recalled or derived, not measured.

3. **The thumb has no rest state against a track (dark is the worst).** In dark the thumb is (164,167,168) on (42,48,52), a bright bar about 122 levels above its surroundings with no track to soften it. It is the brightest non-text, non-accent element in the left half of the window. In light, (106,106,106) on (247,247,247) is closer to native but has the same missing-track problem. Check both against the reference thumb colours per scheme. The pixel values are measured; whether they match the platform needs the reference, not my memory.

4. **The thumb's size fits the content in one scheme but leaves a question in the other.** In light the thumb is 190 of 380px (50%) for about 19 visible rows. In dark it is 164px (43%), so the dark list is longer, which is plausible for a dark-style list. In both frames the list sits partway down (offset 61px light, 52px dark), so these are not top-of-list frames. That is fine, but it means neither Always frame shows the bar's at-rest geometry at scroll position zero, so a top-anchored thumb isn't covered.

Nothing else in the window scrolls. The Preview's Notes mock fits its frame, so its missing bar does not break "every region that scrolls has one".

## What was done with it

- The reply confirms this task's subject: at Always the thumb is drawn at x 312-318, 2 px from the box's trailing edge at x 320, and at When scrolling no thumb is drawn at rest.
- Items 1 and 3: the missing track and the thumb's fill per scheme are the standing bar's measurement, planned as CK10.43 (finding 1137); not filed again.
- Item 2: the lane is 10 px (x 310-320); the 10 px between the rows' end at x 300 and the lane is the box's trailing padding, which this task keeps inside the column on both sides as CK10.33 did for iconbrowser. Whether that padding stays between the rows and the lane at Always is filed as finding 1164. The lane's width is CK10.43's.
- Item 4: not a defect; the column opens with two names above the kept style, as it did before.
