# CK10.50 fresh-eyes review: sk150's page column

Reviewer: a fresh general-purpose agent, briefed with the Scrollbar and Column entries of DOMAIN.md and the full text of reviews/misreads.md, on eight renders of sk150's Presets and Device tabs at Always, light and dark, at 720 x 300 and 720 x 903, at rest, after workbench 6227619.

Disposition: no finding is filed. The one possible violation (a lane kept at 903 px where the content fits and no bar is drawn) is planned under CK10.72, which reads what the platform draws when the content fits and holds that a lane exists only for a bar that is drawn. The crowding between the Device rows and the footer is the Device grid's foot, planned under CK10.53.

## The reviewer's reply, verbatim

I opened all eight pictures and checked the geometry with a pixel probe I wrote myself (PIL is not installed). Coordinates below are in px; the frames are 1x, so px equals dp. I edited, created and committed no files except that probe, which is in the scratchpad.

**presets-light-720x300 and presets-dark-720x300**

The two schemes have identical geometry.
- **Conforms: bar at the window's trailing edge.** The thumb covers x 712–717 and comes within 2 px of the 720 edge. Nothing is inset by the 16 dp padding. This matches "the bar is at the column's trailing edge, never inset by the padding."
- **Conforms: the rows stop short of the lane.** The Recall buttons end at x 689, and the page's 20 dp (16 + 4) separates them from the lane that starts at about x 710. This matches "At Always the bar has a lane of its own and the rows stop short of the lane" and "A page's padding stays between its content and the lane."
- **Conforms: the region runs to the bottom edge and clips.** The rows run to the window's bottom edge, and the M4 row is cut off with no fade. This matches "clipped at the edge, never faded."
- **Conforms: the lane runs only beside the rows.** The thumb starts at y 188, level with the first row (M0, y about 186). It does not run beside the "Memory slots" heading, the sentence under it or the column heads, which stay fixed above. This matches "The lane runs only beside what scrolls."
- **Conforms: header and tab strip stay inset.** The tab strip's line runs from x 16 to x 703, still inset by the page's padding as the brief describes.

**device-light-720x300 and device-dark-720x300**

The two schemes have identical geometry.
- **Conforms: bar at the window's trailing edge.** The thumb is at x 712–717 and starts at y 98, just below the tab strip's line at y 95. The section headings "Input and charging" and "Pane" scroll with the rows, so the region starts at the tab strip. This matches "every region that scrolls has one" and the trailing-edge sentence.
- **Conforms: the footer sits outside the region.** The "Link: 9600 baud …" footer is below the region, at y about 263–278, and does not scroll.
- **Conforms: the last row is clipped, not faded.** The rows are cut off at the region's bottom: the CP row's text and toggle are cut mid-height at about y 258. This is the clip the entry asks for. The fact that no space separates the last row from the footer belongs to the Device grid's foot, which is already planned, so I am not filing it.

**presets-light-720x903 and presets-dark-720x903**

The content fits, and no bar or track is drawn.
- **Possible violation: a lane is still reserved with no bar drawn.** The Recall buttons still end at x 689, exactly where they end at 300 px, where the lane is present. If nothing is drawn, the rows should run to the page padding at x 699. Instead they stop about 10 px further in, short of a lane with nothing in it. The entry says "a lane exists only for a bar that is drawn" and "an empty reserved lane is a margin with no reason."
  - This holds only if the reference records that the platform draws nothing at Always when the content fits. If the reference records an empty standing track, the fault is the other way round: the track should be drawn in this lane. Either way the lane and what is drawn in it disagree, and the stored reference decides which side is wrong.
  - I could not rule out that the Presets grid simply ends at 689, but the identical x at both heights points to a reserved lane. Someone should check this against the reference before acting on it.
- **Conforms: everything else.** The heading, column heads and rows are otherwise correct, and their insets match the 300 px frames.

**device-light-720x903 and device-dark-720x903**

- **Conforms.** The content fits and nothing is drawn at x 714. The footer sits at the window's bottom, outside the rows. The header and tab strip are inset as at 300 px.
- **Cannot be checked: the empty-lane question.** No Device row reaches the trailing edge (the right-hand column ends at about x 635), so these frames cannot show whether a lane is reserved here.

**Matters of taste**

- In the 300 px frames the footer text sits directly under the clipped rows, with no line or space between them. That reads as crowded, but it belongs to the already planned Device grid's foot, so I am not filing it.

**Not reported**

- The thumb's length and position (track and travel) and anything else on the brief's planned list.
- The flat Material-style switches and the substitute font, which are the harness's stand-ins for platform parts.
