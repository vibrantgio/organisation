A general-purpose reviewer read todos' window in both appearances against

## Reply

**Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES.** The delete mark appears in the right states, and it has the right centre and colour family. Its size and stroke are a little off the measured rows, and a row is twice the measured height.

I measured everything below with PIL on the stored PNGs. "Off the fill" means more than 20 levels from the fill, summed over the three channels, which is the method controls.md uses. Coordinates are render pixels; the window's last column is x 649.

1. **States: matches.**
   - At rest (`todos-{light,dark}-list.png`), no pixel in x 450–649 on y 40–300 is off the fill in either appearance. The mark is drawn on no row.
   - With the pointer over row 2 (`todos-{light,dark}-list-pointer.png`), the rest and pointer frames differ only inside x 615–634, y 128–147: 204 pixels in light, 272 in dark. The mark is drawn on the row under the pointer only. The row gets no fill of its own: at (400,138) both frames read (255,255,255) in light and (30,30,30) in dark. This matches the hover row's "the row draws no fill of its own".
   - Todos has no selected row, so the third state of the Action entry does not arise. I did not count its absence against the window.

2. **Geometry and colour against the measured rows.**
   - **Size: 20 × 20 px, against 18 × 18 measured. 2 px too large.** Measured: the bounding box is x 615–634, y 128–147 in both appearances.
   - **Ring stroke: about 3 px, against 2 px measured.** Measured: a run across y 137 reads the ring at x 615–617 and x 632–634; a run down x 624 reads it at y 128–130 and y 145–147. The reference reads 2 px rings (x 596–597 and 612–613). The cross inside is 4–6 px wide, against the reference's "i".
   - **Inset: 15 px, against 16 px measured.** Measured: the mark's last column is x 634, and x 635–649 read the fill, then the window edge. The reference's mark ends 16 px inside the edge, at x 613 of 630. The centre agrees exactly: x 624.5 = 650 − 25.5, and the reference's x 604.5 = 630 − 25.5. The 1 px inset difference comes from the larger size, not from a misplaced centre.
   - **Vertical place.** Measured: the mark's centre is y 137.5, the same as the leading checkbox (y 130–145) and the text line's ink (y 129–146). By eye it is centred on the row's middle. I could not measure it against the row's bounds, because the row draws no fill and so its edges cannot be read from the pixels.
   - **Colour.** Measured: the peak pixel is `#007aff` in both appearances, at (624,129) light and (623,129) dark. The reference's peak reads `#157efb` light and `#1c87fb` dark. `#007aff` is `controlAccentColor` exactly, which is the difference controls.md already lists under "What is open here". This is not a new divergence, and the frame shows a frozen accent, so the colour cannot judge the OS accent wiring.

3. **Row height: about 63 px, against 32 px measured.** Measured: the checkboxes' tops repeat at y 67, 130, 193 and 256, so the row pitch is 63 px. The measured rows give 32 px. This is the one geometry departure larger than a pixel or two. It also means the mark's "6 clear above, 8 below in the 32 px row" relation cannot hold here.

4. **Action entry: the menu-bar half cannot be checked from renders, and may have a gap.** The entry requires every action a control offers to also be in the menu bar with its key equivalent. The headless render has no menu bar, so this needs a check of the window's menu configuration. There is also a real question here: the list selects no row, so a menu-bar Delete has no row to act on. Delete may then be operable only by the pointer, which would be "offered nowhere" for keyboard use. This is worth confirming against the menu wiring.

**Other observations (outside the subject):**
- **Unchecked checkboxes are nearly invisible.** Measured: the darkest pixel of an unchecked box is (243,243,243) on white in light and (44,51,56) on (30,30,30) in dark. The reference's completion circle reads `#bebebe` and `#535353`. The leading control is drawn in every state as the rows require, but at almost no contrast.
- **The bottom-right add button is a floating round button.** That is not a Reminders pattern (Reminders adds through a "+" in the toolbar). In dark, its plus is drawn in the window fill, (30,30,30), on the accent fill (0,122,255), where the platform draws white on accent; light reads white.
