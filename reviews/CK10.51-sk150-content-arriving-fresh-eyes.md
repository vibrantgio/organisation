# CK10.51 fresh-eyes review: sk150's content arriving

Reviewed 2026-10-22 at workbench 776295a: four headless renders of sk150's Presets and Device tabs while their content is arriving, light and dark, at the "when scrolling" bar setting, against the Progress and Empty state entries. The brief included the full text of reviews/misreads.md. The reviewer's reply, verbatim:

---

1. Placement under Progress (Q1): no defect. On both tabs, in both schemes, the sentence is the only thing drawn below the tab strip. No list row, no divider and no row background appear anywhere between y 96 and the bottom edge. A probe of the glyph pixels gives:
   - Presets: ink box (190,488)-(529,514), centre (359.5, 501.0).
   - Device: ink box (182,478)-(538,504), centre (360.0, 491.0).

   Both centres are within 0.5 px of the stated measurements. Against the frame centres, (360, 499.5) and (360, 489.5), the offset is the stated 1.5 to 2 px glyph-versus-line-box bias. Horizontally both are centred to within half a pixel. The sentence is placed as an empty state is, not as a row of the content.

2. Frame choice (Q2): note. Each sentence centres on its list's frame, which is what "the frame the content would occupy" asks for. I see no bar lane, which is right for the "when scrolling" setting. The 10 px difference between the tabs comes from the Device page reserving its footer row and the 20 dp bottom padding. Those are outside the list, so leaving them out is correct by the letter of the entry.

   The catch is that the footer draws nothing while the settings are arriving. The Device render shows nothing at all below y 504. So a viewer sees an empty area running to y 903, and the sentence sits about 10 px above that area's visible centre. I accept it, because the footer is a separate region and not part of the list. Reconsider only if the footer stays undrawn in the arrived state too, since then it is not a region at all.

   Related: the frame starts below the tab strip, not at the window top. The Empty state entry's "the toolbar's share included" (from Mail) does not apply here as I read it. The title row and tab strip are window-level navigation shared by all three tabs, not a toolbar belonging to this column. If the owner reads the header as this column's toolbar share, the centre would move to about y 451. That is the only reading under which the placement is wrong, and I think it is the weaker one.

3. Colour and size: no defect. The darkest glyph pixel is (189,189,189) on white and (86,86,86) on (30,30,30). That matches the platform's tertiary label colour: black at about 26% on white gives about 189, and white at about 25% on (30,30,30) gives about 86. The size is uniform across all four renders and visibly larger than the tab labels and body text, consistent with the 28 sp empty-state size. Roboto in place of SF is a harness substitution and not reported.

4. Text shape (Q3): note, and it is a conflict in the Language, not in the app. The Empty state entry lists "content still arriving" among its conditions and gives its line a shape: title case, no period, stating the condition. The Progress entry says content still arriving "is a task in progress, not an empty state". It borrows only the empty state's placement ("as an empty state does") and calls the text "the application's own sentence".

   So the two entries disagree on whether arriving content is an empty state, and so on whether the title-case shape applies. I read Progress as the more specific entry and the one that governs. Under it, "Reading the memory slots…" and "Reading the device settings…" are acceptable:
   - The ellipsis is the platform's sign for an ongoing process, not a period.
   - Neither line names a control.
   - Both match the app's existing "Waiting for the first reading…".

   What I would do: take "content still arriving" out of the Empty state entry's list of conditions, or replace it with a pointer to Progress. Then add one clause to Progress giving the text shape: a short sentence in sentence case, ending in an ellipsis while the task runs. I would not retitle the two lines to "Reading the Memory Slots…", which would break their consistency with the Monitor tab's line.

5. Wording, "memory slots" (Q3): note. The tab's own name is "Presets", but its waiting line says "the memory slots". If the user meets the device's memory slots nowhere else in the window, that word is the device's term for storage, not the user's word for what the tab shows. "Reading the presets…" would use the vocabulary the window already shows. If the presets are labelled as memory slots elsewhere in the window (M0 to M9, for example), the current wording is fine. "Device settings" on the Device tab has no such mismatch.

6. Anything else (Q4): nothing further. The four renders show no list row, no header row, no stray separator and no second empty state inside the list frame. The missing indeterminate indicator, the header's standing bar and the Device grid's foot are excluded by the brief and not reported. The light and dark renders agree pixel for pixel in geometry.

Verdict: both tabs meet the Progress and Empty state entries for placement, frame and colour with no defects. The open notes are the Language conflict over whether arriving content is an empty state, which needs a ruling, and "memory slots" versus the "Presets" tab name.

---

Disposition: point 4 is filed as finding 1175. Point 5 is answered by the window itself: the Presets list is headed "Memory slots", so the sentence uses the window's own word. Points 1, 2, 3 and 6 need no action.
