# CK10.52 fresh-eyes review: the list's focus ring

Reviewed 2026-10-22 at components e6a8b4a and workbench bb59d1a: four live headless renders of a focused list in vaultview's switch dialog and chooser, light and dark, against the Focused entry, with the same four frames before the change for comparison. The brief included the full text of reviews/misreads.md. The reviewer's reply, verbatim:

---

I found no defects across the four frames. Every claim in the brief matched my pixel probes (PIL on `scratchpad/after/*.png`, compared with `before/`).

**Question 1: is the ring around the box, outside it, never over a row?**

1. **The ring is outside the box in all four frames.** The ring colour fills x 286–289 and 810–813 on the sides. Top and bottom it fills y 307–310 / 503–506 in the picker and y 374–377 / 486–489 in the chooser. Nothing in ring colour falls inside the box (picker x 290–809, y 311–502; chooser x 290–809, y 378–485).
2. **Rows reach the box edge unblended.** In all four frames, columns 290 and 809 contain only two values: the selected fill and the plain row fill.
   - Light: selected (0,100,225), plain (255,255,255).
   - Dark: selected (0,89,209), plain (30,30,30).
   - The selected fill runs y 311–334 in the picker and y 378–413 in the chooser, from its first row to its last.
   - The box's four corner pixels, (290, y0), (809, y0), (290, y1−1) and (809, y1−1), are clean row fill.
   - Before the change, x 290–291 showed blended (0,101,234) in light and (13,129,232) in dark. Plain rows had ring colour at x 290–291 and 808–809. The old overlap is gone.
3. **The change touches nothing else.** A before/after diff bounding box is (286,307)–(814,507) for the picker and (286,374)–(814,490) for the chooser, which is exactly the ring band. The box, the rows, the text and the footer are pixel-identical to before.

**Question 2: width, clipping and clear space**

4. **The ring is 4 px wide on all four sides in all four frames.** It is one flat colour: (128,179,250) in light and (28,99,142) in dark. Only the outermost corner pixels differ; see item 8.
5. **Nothing clips it or draws over it.** All 5,748 ring pixels in the picker and 5,076 in the chooser are either ring colour or the corner antialiasing.
6. **Clear space around the ring:**
   - Picker: 17 px of empty space between the breadcrumb's lowest pixel (y 289) and the ring (y 307). 8 px between the ring's bottom (y 506) and the footer hairline (y 515; light (230,230,230), dark (52,52,52)).
   - Both dialogs: 16 px between the ring and the dialog's left and right edges (the dialog fill spans x 270–829).
   - Chooser: 15 px from the ring's bottom (y 489) to the dialog's bottom edge, where the shadow starts at y 506. 9 px of empty space between the subtitle's lowest pixel (y 364) and the ring (y 374).
   - Everything is adequate. The tightest gap is the chooser's 9 px above the ring, against 15–16 px on its other three sides. The box sits closer to the subtitle than to the dialog's edges. Before the change this gap was 11 px. Note only, not a defect.

**Question 3: anything else**

7. **The list box has square corners.** All four corner pixels are full row fill. A square-cornered list frame with a ring is acceptable for a macOS bordered list or table. Note only.
8. **The ring's outer corners are rounded by about 1 px, its inner corners are square.** At the outer corners, (286,307) is (224,234,253) in light and its neighbours are (143–144,186–187,250). Whether a 4 px ring around a square box should have an outer radius matching its width is a question for the reference. At 1x this is barely visible. Note only.
9. **I did not judge the ring colour.** The reference values were not in the brief, and the misreads file warns that hue impressions off 1x frames are unreliable. In the dark frames the ring reads as a muted blue next to the saturated selected row, and the brief says this comes from flattening the indicator over (30,30,30). If the dark ring colour is a concern, it should be checked against the Voice Memos reference numbers, not by eye.
10. **Against the Focused entry, the frames comply.** The ring is the only thing focus draws. The selected row keeps its selection fill and its white text under focus. The ring is around the list's outer edge and over no row.
