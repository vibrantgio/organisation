   - "search" depicts a magnifying glass.
   - "clear" depicts a filled disc with a cross knocked out.
   - "refresh" depicts a circular arrow.

   Pixel evidence: the labels "search" (about x=91, y=275), "clear" (about x=891, y=155) and "refresh" (about x=891, y=215) in iconbrowser-light-all.png and iconbrowser-dark-all.png.

The other marks follow the rule: check, plus, minus, folder, folder-plus, document, sidebar, the chevrons and square-and-arrow-up are all named by what they depict.
