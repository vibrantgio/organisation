# CK10.33 — iconbrowser's page with the grid scrolling as the column, read cold in both appearances

## What was reviewed

Four whole-window renders at 1000 × 700, 1x, the whole catalogue with no query: light and dark at the platform setting Always, and light and dark at When scrolling with nothing scrolled. The reviewer was briefed with `reviews/misreads.md`, given no checklist, and asked what a developer on macOS would complain about.

## The reply, verbatim

A macOS developer would complain about these, most serious first. I left out everything the misreads file covers: no traffic lights, the empty band above the search field, Roboto instead of SF, the frozen accent, and no scrollbar in the "When scrolling" frames at rest. Positions are pixels in the 1000x700 frames.

1. **"Always" scroll bars are drawn the overlay way (both schemes).** With "Show scroll bars: Always", macOS draws a permanent scroller about 15pt wide, with a track and a seam against the content, and it narrows the content by that width. Here there is no track, no seam and no narrowing. There is only a free-floating grey thumb about 5px wide at x≈992–997, y≈334–350. It sits in the same 13px right margin that the "961 icons" text and the last column use, so it is the "When scrolling" thumb that never fades. The two settings should look clearly different and they don't.

2. **The scrollbar only covers part of the window.** The thumb starts just under the "Material Design icons / 961 icons" header, not under the search field. Either only the Material grid scrolls and the marks section and search field stay pinned above it (nested scrolling), or the thumb is in the wrong place for a view at its top. If it's nested scrolling, the grid gets only about 360 of 700px, and the window doesn't scroll as one document the way Finder or the SF Symbols app does. The thumb is also about 16px long for roughly 160 rows, which is at or below the shortest knob AppKit draws, so it's a tiny target.

3. **The search field is a content-width pill instead of a platform search field.** It's a 976px grey capsule at y 44–72, and its "Search" placeholder is visibly larger than the 13pt text a platform search field uses. It looks bigger than the section headings. A Mac developer expects a toolbar search field at the top right, or at least a standard-height field with 13pt text, not a full-width bar.

4. **Every icon is painted in the accent colour.** The marks and all 961 Material icons use the accent blue, which makes the dark frames especially loud. Icon browsers on the platform (SF Symbols, Xcode's library) show symbols in the label colour and save the accent for selection. With everything in the accent, a selected or focused item would have nothing left to stand out with.

5. **The size scales clash.** The marks are drawn at 16/20/24 (the 16 chevron-down is about 6px wide and nearly unreadable at 1x), right above Material icons at about 36px. The two sections look like different apps, and the small marks are hard to judge next to the large icons.

6. **The section note uses Android and internal wording.** "one mark at 16, 20, 24 dp; chevron-right also turned, for an open row" says "dp" where a Mac developer thinks in pt. "Turned" and "open row" are unexplained, and the fourth symbol makes chevron-right the only cell with four symbols instead of three.

7. **Minor: the cells give no sign that they can be selected or copied.** Nothing in the stills suggests an icon can be clicked or its name copied. That can't be judged from a still, so it's at most a question for a live check, not a finding.

I measured the scrollbar geometry from zoomed crops. The other points are visual reads, and per the misreads file I made no hue claims beyond "accent blue everywhere".

## What was done with it

- Item 1: the thumb is now drawn against the window's trailing edge, which is this task's subject; the missing track and seam and the 10 px lane width are the scrollbar component's, filed as finding 1137.
- Item 2: only the icon grid scrolls; whether the marks and headings scroll with it is filed as finding 1138. The thumb's length is the component's minimum.
- Item 3: CK10.40 already draws the field from the platform's field on content.
- Item 4: filed as finding 1139.
- Items 5 and 6: filed together as finding 1140.
- Item 7: not a finding from a still, as the reviewer says.
